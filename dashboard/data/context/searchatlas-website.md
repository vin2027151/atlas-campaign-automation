### Products

#### OTTO SEO
https://searchatlas.com/otto-seo/ - An AI agent that scans a site and deploys technical, on-page, content, link-building, PR and local SEO optimizations.
- Connects via the OTTO Pixel (header JavaScript), Cloudflare Worker/DNS, or native connectors for WordPress, HubSpot, Webflow, Shopify, Contentful and Duda.
- Submits changed URLs through Google Search Console Instant Indexing and IndexNow (Bing).
- Uses Google Analytics, Search Console and Business Profile data; approval mode, rollback, and "Deep Freeze" to keep optimizations live after cancellation.
- Includes WILDFIRE backlink building and OTTO Grade health scores; states it is not a WordPress plugin and coexists with Yoast or Rank Math.
- Stated stats: "99%" reduction in manual SEO tasks, "100X" faster implementation, "50%" workflow efficiency, "30K+" websites optimized (site claims).
- Included with Search Atlas plans; capacity depends on plan.

#### Atlas Agent
https://searchatlas.com/atlas-agent/ - A conversational AI agent inside Search Atlas that plans and executes marketing workflows across SEO, PPC, content, authority, local, reporting and AI visibility.
- Runs pre-built or custom Playbooks with approval checkpoints; builds 30-90 day growth plans.
- Stated stats: "99%" reduction in manual SEO tasks, "90+" day strategies in 30 seconds, "10X" faster deployment, "1st" full-stack SEO agent for Vibe SEO (site claims).
- Included in all plans, operating through OTTO SEO activations; "no separate fees".
- 7-day free trial.

#### LLM Visibility
https://searchatlas.com/llm-visibility/ - Tracks brand mentions, citations, share of voice, sentiment and answer placement in AI-generated answers.
- Platforms named vary on the page: ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, Gemini; also Claude, Grok, SearchGPT in FAQs. Home: Claude on Pro and Agency; Pro lists "6 AI platforms".
- Flags an opportunity when a competitor's visibility score exceeds yours by more than 25%; fixes dispatched via OTTO SEO.
- Stated stats: "40%" boost in AI share of voice, "2B+" monthly AI brand mentions monitored (site claims).
- "Included in plans from $99/mo with 7-day trial". Home plan limits: Starter 1 project, 1 provider, 500 credits; Growth 3,500 credits; Pro 20,000; Agency 50,000 and unlimited projects.

#### Smart Ads
https://searchatlas.com/smart-ads/ - Runs Google Ads accounts through the Atlas Agent: builds campaigns, audits, reallocates budget.
- Creates search and retargeting campaigns (audiences built automatically), imports existing campaigns; Campaign Hub for multiple accounts.
- Health score audit, negative keyword cleanup, budget pacing, reports on cost, conversions, CPA, ROAS.
- Fast mode or Advanced mode (step-by-step approvals); "No changes are published without your approval."
- Stated stats: "18%" CTR on launch, "40+" more return, "99%" costs saved, "200+" ads in minutes (site claims).
- Page says included in Starter, Growth, Pro and Agency; home labels Google Ads "from Growth".

#### Meta Ad Studio
https://searchatlas.com/meta-ad-studio/ - Creates Facebook and Instagram ads from brand assets, competitor examples and a prompt, and launches them through Meta.
- Discover: browse and save competitor ads by industry and format (image ads; video adaptation listed as roadmap).
- BrandVault (logo, colors, photos, voice), Creative Drive (storage, folders, ratings, comments), Launchpad (campaign assembly from a prompt).
- Campaigns publish to Meta paused for review.
- No numbers, limits or prices stated on the page; home labels Meta Ads "from Growth".

#### Content Genius
https://searchatlas.com/content-genius/ - Researches the live SERP, builds a brief and topical map, and writes publish-ready SEO articles.
- Uses "15+" specialized microagents (research, SEO, formatting, visuals, localization); multilingual.
- Semantic Grader; proposes Article, FAQ, How-To and Product schema; internal link suggestions.
- Article time stated variously as "15 minutes", "10-15 minutes", "9-15 minutes" (site claims); "95%" SEO score consistency, "Trained on 10,000 Google patents" (site claims).
- Publishing formats: WordPress, Medium, LinkedIn and others.
- Page says included in plans "without hidden add-ons or credit systems"; home plan examples count premium AI articles against Universal Credits.

#### GBP Galactic
https://searchatlas.com/gbp-galactic/ - Manages Google Business Profiles: listing updates, review replies, Q&A, posts and schema across locations.
- AI review replies, scheduled GBP posts, auto-generated Q&A, NAP and hours/category sync.
- "1 to 100+ profiles"; connects via Google OAuth; runs from OTTO's GBP Optimization tab.
- Connected with Citation Builder, Local SERPs Viewer, Local Heatmap.
- Included in subscription; "Each plan comes with a set number of GBP Galactic projects."

#### Website Studio
https://searchatlas.com/website-studio/ - Builds and publishes marketing pages from a plain-language brief.
- Clone Website Mode, plain-language and visual edits, version restore, lead capture forms.
- GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and custom scripts; static CDN hosting, auto SSL, custom domain via CNAME.
- "90+" Lighthouse scores, "10X" faster page production (site claims). White-label for agencies.
- Pricing on page: Starter ($99/month), Growth ($199/month), Pro ($399/month), Agency ($999/month).

#### White Label SEO Software
https://searchatlas.com/white-label-seo-software/ - Runs the Search Atlas platform under an agency's logo, domain and colors, with client portals and reporting.
- Custom URL via CNAME/DNS; per-project client permissions; Search Atlas branding, live chat and help links removed.
- Scheduled white-label PDF and web reports from a custom email domain; "50+ SEO and LLM capabilities".
- Site Auditor crawls "up to 1 million web pages"; "5 billion-keyword database".
- "1,400+" agency partners, "28,000+" client portals (site claims).
- White labeling "included in every Pro plan"; client activations billed at "70% discounted rates".

#### Enterprise Agency
https://searchatlas.com/enterprise-agency/ - Custom-quoted plan for agencies managing 25 to 1,000+ client sites.
- Target: "25 - 1,000+" websites or GBP accounts, "$50,000 - $250,000+" Google ad spend.
- Agency plan "starts at $999/month billed annually ($11,988/year) or $999 month-to-month, covering 10 projects, 1,000,000 Universal Credits, and 10 team seats"; enterprise quotes add credit pooling, dedicated account management, API access.
- Includes Signal Genesys (press release distribution), Report Builder with AI summaries, white-label dashboards.
- Imports data from Semrush, Ahrefs and SpyFu.
- Stated portfolio results: "+30%" organic traffic, "+90%" keyword rankings within 90 days, "12+ hours/week saved" (site claims).
